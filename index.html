Data & privacy

FitTrack stores logs locally in your browser. There is no account, analytics, advertising, cloud sync or external API in this starter app. On a shared device, other people with access to your browser profile may be able to see the data.

Fitness tracking is for general wellness, not diagnosis. Avoid treating heart-rate or recovery metrics as medical advice. Increase training gradually and seek medical advice for concerning symptoms.
